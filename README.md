# Korean Learning Website for Mandarin & Japanese Speakers

An interactive website designed to help complete beginners learn Korean, specifically tailored for those who are fluent in Mandarin Chinese and Japanese.

## Features

### 1. Hangul Basics (한글)
- Learn Korean vowels and consonants
- Pronunciation guides with comparisons to Mandarin and Japanese sounds
- Interactive character cards (click to see animations)
- Practice combining letters into syllables

### 2. Vocabulary (词汇/単語)
- **Sino-Korean Words**: 60% of Korean vocabulary comes from Chinese! Learn words that share the same roots with Mandarin and Japanese
- **Pure Korean Words**: Native Korean vocabulary with translations in both Chinese and Japanese
- Click "Mark as Learned" to track your progress

### 3. Grammar (文法)
- **Word Order**: Korean uses SOV structure, same as Japanese!
- **Particles**: Learn Korean particles with direct comparisons to Japanese particles (は, が, を, の)
- **Politeness Levels**: Understanding Korean honorifics (similar to Japanese keigo)

### 4. Interactive Quiz (测验/クイズ)
- Test your knowledge with 12+ questions
- Instant feedback and explanations
- Track your score and progress

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Navigate through different sections using the tabs at the top
3. Click on Hangul cards to see animations
4. Mark vocabulary as learned to track progress
5. Take the quiz to test your knowledge
6. Your progress is displayed in the progress bar at the top

## Key Learning Points

### For Mandarin Speakers (中文使用者)
- 60% of Korean vocabulary is Sino-Korean (한자어)
- Many words share the same Chinese characters
- Example: 学校 (xuéxiào) = 학교 (hak-gyo)

### For Japanese Speakers (日本語話者)
- Korean grammar is almost identical to Japanese
- Same SOV word order
- Similar particle system (은/는 ≈ は, 이/가 ≈ が, 을/를 ≈ を)
- Similar politeness levels (합니다체 ≈ です/ます, 해요체 ≈ everyday polite, 반말 ≈ plain form)

### Best Strategy
- Use your **Chinese knowledge** for learning vocabulary
- Use your **Japanese knowledge** for understanding grammar
- This dual approach will accelerate your Korean learning!
